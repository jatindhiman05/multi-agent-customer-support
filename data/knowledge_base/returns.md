# VoltNest Return Policy

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery.

Returned products must be in their original condition and include all accessories supplied with the product.

Products damaged through misuse, unauthorized modification, or accidental damage are not eligible for standard returns.

Once a returned product is received and inspected, an approved refund is issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing may take 5–10 business days after approval.
