# Limited Warranty Policy

VoltNest products include a one-year limited warranty beginning on the original purchase date unless a product is explicitly sold with different warranty terms.

## Covered issues

The limited warranty covers qualifying defects in materials or workmanship arising during normal intended use.

## Not covered

The limited warranty does not normally cover:

- Accidental damage.
- Damage caused by misuse or improper handling.
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs.
- Normal cosmetic wear.
- Loss or theft.
- Damage caused by use outside the product's documented operating requirements.

## Warranty resolution

Depending on the circumstances, an eligible warranty claim may result in troubleshooting, repair, replacement, or another resolution determined by VoltNest.

## Proof of purchase

VoltNest may require verification of the original purchase or associated order before processing warranty service.

## Return policy relationship

The warranty and return policy are separate.

A product outside the standard return period may still qualify for warranty service if the warranty remains active and the issue is covered.

## Product-specific terms

Some products may have additional warranty requirements. Product-specific documentation takes precedence when explicitly stated.
