# Damaged or Incorrect Items Policy

Customers should contact VoltNest Support if an order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or contains an incorrect product.

## What to provide

VoltNest may request information necessary to investigate the issue, including:

- The affected order.
- The product involved.
- A description of the problem.
- Relevant photographs or other supporting information when appropriate.

## Resolution

Available resolutions depend on the order and issue and may include replacement, return, refund, troubleshooting, or escalation to a human support specialist.

## Packaging

Customers should retain the product and relevant packaging while the issue is being reviewed when reasonably possible.

## Safety concerns

Customers should stop using a product if continued use appears unsafe.

A safety-related product issue should be escalated for appropriate review rather than handled as an ordinary return question.

## Specific orders

VoltNest Support must inspect live order information before promising a specific replacement, refund, or other resolution.
