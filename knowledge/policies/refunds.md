# Refund Policy

VoltNest issues eligible refunds to the original payment method whenever possible.

## When refunds are issued

A refund may be issued after:

- An eligible return has been approved and processed.
- An eligible paid order has been cancelled.
- VoltNest determines that a refund is appropriate for a supported order issue.

## Refund processing

Once VoltNest successfully initiates a refund, the time required for the funds to appear depends on the payment provider and the customer's financial institution.

Customers should not interpret a successfully initiated refund as an immediate bank balance update.

## Original payment method

Refunds are normally returned to the original payment method used for the purchase.

## Shipping charges

Original shipping charges are not automatically refundable unless required by the circumstances of the order or applicable policy.

## Duplicate refunds

VoltNest does not issue multiple refunds for the same refundable payment.

## Specific refund status

This document describes general refund policy only.

For the status or amount of a refund associated with a specific order, VoltNest Support must use the customer's live order and payment information.
