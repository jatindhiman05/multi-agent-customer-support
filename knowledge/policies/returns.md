# Returns Policy

VoltNest accepts returns of eligible products within 30 calendar days of delivery.

## Eligibility

A product is eligible for return when:

- The return request is submitted within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- The product was purchased directly from VoltNest.
- The order can be verified in the customer's account.
- The product is not classified as non-returnable.

## Product condition

Products should be returned with their original accessories and components when reasonably possible.

Products that are materially damaged through misuse, unauthorized modification, or improper handling may not qualify for a standard return.

## Non-returnable items

The following may not qualify for a standard return:

- Products returned after the 30-day return window.
- Products that have been materially modified by the customer.
- Products damaged through misuse or unauthorized repair.
- Items explicitly identified as final sale or non-returnable at the time of purchase.

A defective product may still qualify for warranty service even when it is outside the standard return window.

## Refunds

Approved returns are refunded according to the VoltNest Refund Policy.

Refund timing depends on the original payment method and the customer's financial institution.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Customers who receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product should contact VoltNest Support rather than submitting a standard return without explanation.

## Questions about a specific order

Return eligibility for a specific order depends on live order information. VoltNest Support may need to inspect the customer's order before confirming eligibility.
