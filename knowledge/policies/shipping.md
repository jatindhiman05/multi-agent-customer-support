# Shipping Policy

VoltNest processes eligible orders for shipment after successful order and payment processing.

## Processing

Order processing and shipment are separate stages.

An order marked as processing has not necessarily been handed to a shipping carrier.

## Tracking

When tracking information is available, customers can obtain the carrier, tracking number, shipment status, and available delivery updates through VoltNest Support.

## Estimated delivery

Estimated delivery dates are estimates and may change because of carrier operations, weather, destination conditions, or other fulfillment circumstances.

## Shipping delays

A delayed shipment does not necessarily indicate that the package is lost.

Customers should review the latest tracking event before reporting a shipment as missing.

## Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery address when placing an order.

Once fulfillment has progressed, changing the delivery address may not be possible.

## Live shipment information

This policy does not contain the current location of individual packages.

VoltNest Support must use live shipment information when answering questions about a specific order.
