# Lost Package Policy

A shipment should not automatically be considered lost merely because delivery is delayed.

## Delayed shipments

Customers should first review the latest available tracking information and estimated delivery status.

Carrier delays and temporary gaps between tracking events can occur during normal transportation.

## Delivered but not found

If tracking indicates that a package was delivered but the customer cannot locate it, the customer should check the delivery location and other reasonable places where the carrier may have left the package.

## Investigation

When a shipment appears to be lost or cannot be located after a reported delivery, VoltNest Support may escalate the case for investigation.

## Resolution

VoltNest does not promise a refund or replacement solely on the basis of a customer's initial lost-package report.

The appropriate resolution depends on the order, shipment information, investigation, and applicable policy.

## Specific shipment status

VoltNest Support must use live tracking information when determining the current status of a customer's shipment.
