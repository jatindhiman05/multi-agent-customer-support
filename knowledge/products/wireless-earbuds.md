# VoltNest Wireless Earbuds

SKU: VN-EARBUDS-001

VoltNest Wireless Earbuds are wireless earbuds supplied with a charging case.

## Connectivity

The earbuds connect to compatible devices using Bluetooth.

To pair the earbuds:

1. Remove both earbuds from the charging case.
2. Enable Bluetooth on the device you want to connect.
3. Open the device's Bluetooth settings.
4. Select "VoltNest Wireless Earbuds" from the available devices.
5. Wait for the device to report that pairing is complete.

## Reconnecting

After successful pairing, the earbuds normally attempt to reconnect to the most recently paired compatible device when removed from the charging case.

If they do not reconnect, open the device's Bluetooth settings and select the earbuds manually.

## Charging

Place both earbuds in the charging case to charge them.

Ensure that the earbuds are seated correctly in the case if charging does not begin.

The charging case must itself have sufficient charge to recharge the earbuds.

## Troubleshooting connection problems

If the earbuds do not appear in the device's Bluetooth list:

1. Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled on the device.
2. Place both earbuds back in the charging case.
3. Remove them again and retry pairing.
4. Remove any existing VoltNest Wireless Earbuds pairing from the device and attempt pairing again.
5. Restart Bluetooth on the device before retrying.

If the issue continues after these steps, contact VoltNest Support.

## One earbud is not working

Place both earbuds in the charging case and confirm that each earbud is seated correctly.

Remove both earbuds and reconnect them to the device.

If one earbud continues to produce no audio after reconnecting, contact VoltNest Support for further troubleshooting.

## Warranty

The VoltNest Wireless Earbuds are covered by the standard VoltNest Limited Warranty unless different terms were explicitly provided with the product.

The warranty covers qualifying defects in materials or workmanship during normal intended use.

Accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modification, normal cosmetic wear, loss, and theft are not covered by the standard limited warranty.

## Safety

Stop using the earbuds if they appear physically damaged, unusually hot, swollen, or otherwise unsafe.

Contact VoltNest Support for a safety-related product issue rather than continuing normal troubleshooting.
